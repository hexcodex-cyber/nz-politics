Simeon Brown

National: Minister of Health; Minister for Energy; State Owned Enterprises; National campaign chair (since April 2026)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “And it also highlights where further work and further investment is needed to make sure that we are seeing improvements across the country.”

    17 October 2026 · Told RNZ, on emergency department figures showing a national improvement but about half of hospitals going backwards (published 17 Oct; interview date not stated). · RNZ

  2. “Making your own power should be as easy as plugging in a toaster.”

    15 October 2026 · As National's energy spokesperson, announcing that shops would be able to sell plug-in solar panels within six months of a re-elected National government. · National Party

  3. “The claim is false. No child has had their cancer care suspended, and no child has missed out on care.”

    15 October 2026 · As Health Minister, responding to reports that nursing shortages had suspended some cancer treatment at Starship children's hospital. A mother told RNZ her toddler's treatment had been delayed. · Te Ao Māori News

  4. “The Advertising Standards Authority has not said KiwiSaver wouldn't be affected by Labour's capital gains tax - it has only suggested that KiwiSaver may not be impacted to the same extent as rental properties, baches and businesses.”

    9 September 2026 · Responding after the ASA partly upheld a complaint that a National attack ad on Labour's CGT was misleading. · RNZ

  5. “Kiwis remember what life was like under Labour – inflation peaking, crime soaring, and public services like health and education going backwards.”

    2 April 2026 · LinkedIn post on becoming campaign chair. · LinkedIn (Simeon Brown)

  6. “I'm focused on making sure that we win in November and making sure that we deliver a National-led government.”

    2 April 2026 · On taking over as campaign chair; asked his experience, he replied "winning elections". · RNZ

  7. “I have never been part of a conversation where that has been said to our prime minister.”

    2 April 2026 · Asked repeatedly whether he had been in a conversation telling Luxon he was losing caucus support. · RNZ

  8. “These changes are about ensuring treatments are safe and carefully managed, while maintaining access to care for those who need it.”

    20 November 2025 · As Health Minister, announcing a pause on new puberty-blocker prescriptions for gender dysphoria pending a UK trial. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Minister of Health; Minister for Energy; State Owned Enterprises; National campaign chair (since April 2026)
Electorate / list
MP for Pakuranga (since 2017); contesting Pakuranga
Background
Elected in 2017. Minister of Energy, Transport and Local Government from 2023. Moved to Health in January 2025. Regained Energy and became campaign chair in the April 2026 reshuffle.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Campaigns hard against Labour's record and its capital gains tax (including the ASA-reviewed ad).
  • As Health Minister, paused new puberty-blocker prescriptions for gender dysphoria in November 2025, pending a UK trial.
