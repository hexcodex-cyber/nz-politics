Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We are sick and tired as we think most New Zealanders are of the incessant attack ads, the AI slop, the taking us out of context - we are interested in a good clean fight.”

    29 October 2026 · Responding to Luxon's post warning of her as finance minister, at a Workers First Union meeting in Auckland. · RNZ

  2. “We are in the Green Party very, very committed to our values and our principles, which look like appropriate decision making, which look like restorative justice, which look like a commitment to evidence-based policy.”

    24 October 2026 · Armageddon Expo, Auckland, asked whether she was comfortable with the standard Hipkins has set on gangs (NZ Herald report via Newstalk ZB). · Newstalk ZB / NZ Herald

  3. “The point of this exercise was just to demonstrate that something can sound really good on the political hustings when it comes to taking tax off this or that but it is really important we have thorough economic analysis so people understand the distributional impacts.”

    22 October 2026 · On Green-commissioned analysis of ACT's KiwiSaver tax policy. · RNZ

  4. “Imagine being required to spend 30 hours a week working and not being paid for it. That's messed up. It doesn't have to be that way. The Greens will fix it.”

    16 October 2026 · On unpaid placements for trainee nurses, teachers and social workers, launching the Greens' paid placements policy. · RNZ

  5. “We can afford to ensure that everybody retires with dignity, and that means that we have to fix the tax system.”

    13 October 2026 · Responding to National's fiscal plan and superannuation stance (reported by RNZ) · RNZ

  6. “He's talking about the Hunger Games!”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, after Seymour defended philanthropy funding public institutions. · 1News

  7. “Someone would have to pay their taxes fairly. That is the case with all New Zealanders who have income taxes that they have to pay.”

    22 June 2026 · 1News Breakfast, asked whether someone could be forced to sell an inherited asset to pay the proposed inheritance tax. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
