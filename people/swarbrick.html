Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “They don't get to sit on the fence any more, and I would want them to choose positivity and optimism for the future of our country.”

    8 November 2026 · Sunday after election night, welcoming Opportunity into Parliament but saying it was 'time to pick a side'. · 1News

  2. “It's normal for a number of young people to want to go on their OE. But what we've seen in the last three years is not normal.”

    2 November 2026 · Releasing Green-commissioned research on the economic cost of young people emigrating. · RNZ

  3. “higher mortgage prices cannot reopen the Strait of Hormuz, only a responsible government can reduce our exposure to imported fossil fuel inflation”

    28 October 2026 · Responding to the Reserve Bank's split decision to hold the OCR at 2.75% (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “Children in poverty cannot eat a surplus.”

    23 October 2026 · Releasing the Green Party's fiscal plan at Parliament. · RNZ

  5. “Imagine being required to spend 30 hours a week working and not being paid for it. That's messed up. It doesn't have to be that way. The Greens will fix it.”

    16 October 2026 · On unpaid placements for trainee nurses, teachers and social workers, launching the Greens' paid placements policy. · RNZ

  6. “This is not so much a cost of living crisis as it is a cost of greed crisis, and the Greens are ready, willing and able to take on corporate vested interests.”

    13 October 2026 · Same Multi-Party Debate exchange. · 1News

  7. “The polls are going to continue to bounce around at the margins over the next few weeks, and this is why it's really important to understand that nothing is set in stone.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to a 1News–Verian poll with the Greens on 16%; she said the Greens would "very clearly" work with Labour. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
