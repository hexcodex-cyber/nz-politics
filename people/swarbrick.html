Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Low pay, insecure work, and a lack of pastoral support mean that instead of focusing on learning their trade, our apprentices spend too much time worried about making ends meet. We can fix that.”

    17 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to abolish the training wage (80% of the minimum wage) for apprentices and trainees. · RNZ

  2. “From Auckland to Queenstown, mayors don't care about central government politicians' squabbles about whether a levy is a tax. They want the tools to fund the infrastructure tourism requires and we will ensure they have them.”

    14 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to let councils set up a bed levy. · RNZ

  3. “I think it's clear that the Green Party want to change the government. We are not interested in reinstalling Christopher Luxon as Prime Minister.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, asked what separates the Greens from Opportunity. · 1News

  4. “We could just implement a wealth tax so that we don't have to have our public services relying on the charity and therefore the discretion, the choices of incredibly wealthy people about who survives and who does not.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to ACT's plan for a philanthropically backed cancer hospital. · 1News

  5. “We will ensure Government investment and incentives drive us where we all want to go: higher wages, sustained research and innovation, better education, resilient infrastructure, healthy nature and a country we can be proud of.”

    13 October 2026 · Same Greens economic development plan announcement. · RNZ

  6. “The polls are going to continue to bounce around at the margins over the next few weeks, and this is why it's really important to understand that nothing is set in stone.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to a 1News–Verian poll with the Greens on 16%; she said the Greens would "very clearly" work with Labour. · 1News

  7. “People aren't dumb. They know that while their cost of living has gone through the roof, corporate profits have skyrocketed and the wealthiest 150 rich listers now own more than half of the country.”

    21 June 2026 · Launching the Greens' tax package (wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes; income-tax cuts for 96%). · RNZ

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
