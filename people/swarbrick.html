Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The point of this exercise was just to demonstrate that something can sound really good on the political hustings when it comes to taking tax off this or that but it is really important we have thorough economic analysis so people understand the distributional impacts.”

    22 October 2026 · On Green-commissioned analysis of ACT's KiwiSaver tax policy. · RNZ

  2. “Low pay, insecure work, and a lack of pastoral support mean that instead of focusing on learning their trade, our apprentices spend too much time worried about making ends meet. We can fix that.”

    17 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to abolish the training wage (80% of the minimum wage) for apprentices and trainees. · RNZ

  3. “From Auckland to Queenstown, mayors don't care about central government politicians' squabbles about whether a levy is a tax. They want the tools to fund the infrastructure tourism requires and we will ensure they have them.”

    14 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to let councils set up a bed levy. · RNZ

  4. “I think it's clear that the Green Party want to change the government. We are not interested in reinstalling Christopher Luxon as Prime Minister.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, asked what separates the Greens from Opportunity. · 1News

  5. “He's talking about the Hunger Games!”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, after Seymour defended philanthropy funding public institutions. · 1News

  6. “The polls are going to continue to bounce around at the margins over the next few weeks, and this is why it's really important to understand that nothing is set in stone.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to a 1News–Verian poll with the Greens on 16%; she said the Greens would "very clearly" work with Labour. · 1News

  7. “Congratulations, they are incredibly lucky to be an individual that is inheriting an excess of $2 million.”

    22 June 2026 · Same interview, on a hypothetical heir to a $2m investment property. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
