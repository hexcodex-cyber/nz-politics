Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The problem with a ban for under 16s is what happens when you turn 17. It is not fair for us solely to focus on users and allow platforms off the hook.”

    5 November 2026 · At the Q+A People's Debate, opposing an under-16 social media ban. · 1News

  2. “These staggering rollbacks put him in the Donald Trump league of climate deniers.”

    30 October 2026 · Green Party press release on the Climate Action Tracker's assessment of New Zealand's climate policy. · RNZ live blog

  3. “These are structural barriers and it doesn't have to be this way. No politician and no government should ever make it harder for anybody to engage in our democracy.”

    25 October 2026 · On the Government ending enrolment in the last two weeks before election day. · RNZ

  4. “The point of this exercise was just to demonstrate that something can sound really good on the political hustings when it comes to taking tax off this or that but it is really important we have thorough economic analysis so people understand the distributional impacts.”

    22 October 2026 · On Green-commissioned analysis of ACT's KiwiSaver tax policy. · RNZ

  5. “From Auckland to Queenstown, mayors don't care about central government politicians' squabbles about whether a levy is a tax. They want the tools to fund the infrastructure tourism requires and we will ensure they have them.”

    14 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to let councils set up a bed levy. · RNZ

  6. “We could just implement a wealth tax so that we don't have to have our public services relying on the charity and therefore the discretion, the choices of incredibly wealthy people about who survives and who does not.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to ACT's plan for a philanthropically backed cancer hospital. · 1News

  7. “The polls are going to continue to bounce around at the margins over the next few weeks, and this is why it's really important to understand that nothing is set in stone.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to a 1News–Verian poll with the Greens on 16%; she said the Greens would "very clearly" work with Labour. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
