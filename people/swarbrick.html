Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “It's normal for a number of young people to want to go on their OE. But what we've seen in the last three years is not normal.”

    2 November 2026 · Releasing Green-commissioned research on the economic cost of young people emigrating. · RNZ

  2. “We are sick and tired as we think most New Zealanders are of the incessant attack ads, the AI slop, the taking us out of context - we are interested in a good clean fight.”

    29 October 2026 · Responding to Luxon's post warning of her as finance minister, at a Workers First Union meeting in Auckland. · RNZ

  3. “Someone is profiting handsomely from driving people into more and more conflict.”

    23 October 2026 · Morning Report, on threats and abuse directed at MPs and the role of social media algorithms (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “Any politician in any political party in any government that is putting up unnecessary barriers to people engaging in the political process is actively engaging in voter suppression, especially when there is not a leg to stand on when it comes to rationale as to why.”

    21 October 2026 · Speaking to reporters in central Auckland about the end of same-day enrolment (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “The way that we best do that is that we understand what is driving that in the first place and we stop that cycle.”

    14 October 2026 · Asked whether victims of gang violence would be upset by Green candidates attending a Mongrel Mob election hui; on ending cycles of violence and criminality. · RNZ

  6. “We could just implement a wealth tax so that we don't have to have our public services relying on the charity and therefore the discretion, the choices of incredibly wealthy people about who survives and who does not.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to ACT's plan for a philanthropically backed cancer hospital. · 1News

  7. “Someone would have to pay their taxes fairly. That is the case with all New Zealanders who have income taxes that they have to pay.”

    22 June 2026 · 1News Breakfast, asked whether someone could be forced to sell an inherited asset to pay the proposed inheritance tax. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
