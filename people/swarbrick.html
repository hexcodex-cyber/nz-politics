Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The polls are going to continue to bounce around at the margins over the next few weeks, and this is why it's really important to understand that nothing is set in stone.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to a 1News–Verian poll with the Greens on 16%; she said the Greens would "very clearly" work with Labour. · 1News

  2. “Someone would have to pay their taxes fairly. That is the case with all New Zealanders who have income taxes that they have to pay.”

    22 June 2026 · 1News Breakfast, asked whether someone could be forced to sell an inherited asset to pay the proposed inheritance tax. · 1News

  3. “Congratulations, they are incredibly lucky to be an individual that is inheriting an excess of $2 million.”

    22 June 2026 · Same interview, on a hypothetical heir to a $2m investment property. · 1News

  4. “People aren't dumb. They know that while their cost of living has gone through the roof, corporate profits have skyrocketed and the wealthiest 150 rich listers now own more than half of the country.”

    21 June 2026 · Launching the Greens' tax package (wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes; income-tax cuts for 96%). · RNZ

  5. “We must electrify everything we can. We need homegrown, sustainable resilience in our energy system, powering everything we do.”

    19 April 2026 · Greens' State of the Planet address calling for a National Electrification Plan during the fuel crisis. · RNZ

  6. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
