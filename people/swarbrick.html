Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We are in the Green Party very, very committed to our values and our principles, which look like appropriate decision making, which look like restorative justice, which look like a commitment to evidence-based policy.”

    24 October 2026 · Armageddon Expo, Auckland, asked whether she was comfortable with the standard Hipkins has set on gangs (NZ Herald report via Newstalk ZB). · Newstalk ZB / NZ Herald

  2. “Come on Nicola, let's get our credentials and credibility and put them to the test. If you've got anything to say about our plan, which I'm sure you will, come and say it to my face.”

    23 October 2026 · Challenging Nicola Willis to a fiscal debate. · RNZ

  3. “Low pay, insecure work, and a lack of pastoral support mean that instead of focusing on learning their trade, our apprentices spend too much time worried about making ends meet. We can fix that.”

    17 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to abolish the training wage (80% of the minimum wage) for apprentices and trainees. · RNZ

  4. “The way that we best do that is that we understand what is driving that in the first place and we stop that cycle.”

    14 October 2026 · Asked whether victims of gang violence would be upset by Green candidates attending a Mongrel Mob election hui; on ending cycles of violence and criminality. · RNZ

  5. “This is not so much a cost of living crisis as it is a cost of greed crisis, and the Greens are ready, willing and able to take on corporate vested interests.”

    13 October 2026 · Same Multi-Party Debate exchange. · 1News

  6. “We will ensure Government investment and incentives drive us where we all want to go: higher wages, sustained research and innovation, better education, resilient infrastructure, healthy nature and a country we can be proud of.”

    13 October 2026 · Same Greens economic development plan announcement. · RNZ

  7. “Congratulations, they are incredibly lucky to be an individual that is inheriting an excess of $2 million.”

    22 June 2026 · Same interview, on a hypothetical heir to a $2m investment property. · 1News

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
