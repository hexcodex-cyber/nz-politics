Chlöe Swarbrick

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “They don't get to sit on the fence any more, and I would want them to choose positivity and optimism for the future of our country.”

    8 November 2026 · Sunday after election night, welcoming Opportunity into Parliament but saying it was 'time to pick a side'. · 1News

  2. “If we end up with TOP choosing the side of those who have the greatest policy alignment, and I believe the greatest values alignment, then we have the opportunity to form a government that centres people and planet, as opposed to extracting from and exploiting both.”

    8 November 2026 · The same Sunday interview, on a possible path to a government if Opportunity sided with the left. · 1News

  3. “We're not interested in measuring up the curtains or whatever other turn of phrase you want to use.”

    6 November 2026 · Downplaying deputy-PM speculation at Kindness Collective. · The Spinoff

  4. “We are sick and tired as we think most New Zealanders are of the incessant attack ads, the AI slop, the taking us out of context - we are interested in a good clean fight.”

    29 October 2026 · Responding to Luxon's post warning of her as finance minister, at a Workers First Union meeting in Auckland. · RNZ

  5. “Come on Nicola, let's get our credentials and credibility and put them to the test. If you've got anything to say about our plan, which I'm sure you will, come and say it to my face.”

    23 October 2026 · Challenging Nicola Willis to a fiscal debate. · RNZ

  6. “From Auckland to Queenstown, mayors don't care about central government politicians' squabbles about whether a levy is a tax. They want the tools to fund the infrastructure tourism requires and we will ensure they have them.”

    14 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' policy to let councils set up a bed levy. · RNZ

  7. “We will ensure Government investment and incentives drive us where we all want to go: higher wages, sustained research and innovation, better education, resilient infrastructure, healthy nature and a country we can be proud of.”

    13 October 2026 · Same Greens economic development plan announcement. · RNZ

  8. “No-one is hoarding, attacking, or starting wars over sun, wind, water and geothermal energy. They don't come through the Strait of Hormuz.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Green Party co-leader
Electorate / list
MP for Auckland Central (since 2020); contesting Auckland Central
Background
Entered Parliament in 2017 as a list MP, won Auckland Central in 2020, and became co-leader in March 2024.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wealth, inheritance and corporate taxes, with income-tax cuts for most people.
  • Electrify 'everything we can'. Will work with Labour to form a government.
