Willie Jackson

Labour: Senior MP; Māori development spokesperson (as reported)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “She did not want to get in the way of us trying to get rid of this rotten government, and she knew the Nats would come after her if she stayed a candidate.”

    23 October 2026 · Facebook post on Leoni's resignation, as Labour's Māori campaign chair (reported by RNZ). · RNZ

  2. “You do not hand the government something, who are absolutely desperate at the moment, desperate, and then all of a sudden you go and have a campaign meeting with the, come on, with the Mob”

    15 October 2026 · RNZ Morning Report, as Labour's Māori electorate campaign chair, saying the Mongrel Mob hui was not a good strategic move by Green and Te Pāti Māori candidates. · RNZ

  3. “Chippy's not saying we're not going to support those families, of course we are. But we want to win an election here.”

    15 October 2026 · Same interview. He said Labour and Hipkins 'abhor' gangs but Labour would not walk away from supporting whānau in gangs. · RNZ

  4. “We've got Māori youth unemployment at a record high. I think it's between 15 and 24. We're up around 25 percent.”

    14 October 2026 · Mata with Mihingarangi Forbes discussion with Paul Goldsmith; Jackson also called Crown–Māori relations an all-time low. Published 14 Oct; recording date not given. · RNZ

  5. “I'm not a fool in terms of how hard it is to knock out incumbents.”

    20 August 2026 · RNZ interview on Labour's chances of winning all seven Māori electorates. · RNZ

  6. “We weren't talking about voting for an overhang. We weren't talking about voting for people who had different politics from us, all we were talking about was using the system to get more Māori through Labour into the Parliament.”

    20 August 2026 · Responding to Debbie Ngarewa-Packer's charge that Labour used a similar strategy in 2017. · RNZ

  7. “You can buy into that strategy if every everything's working well, but they're not even voting for themselves.”

    18 August 2026 · Criticising Te Pāti Māori's electorate-vote-only strategy, which he warned could invite a backlash against the Māori seats. · RNZ

  8. “Nobody believes her. No one who is aware of the situation, who followed the situation, believes her.”

    16 August 2026 · On Erica Stanford's denial of involvement in the National leadership challenge. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Senior MP; Māori development spokesperson (as reported)
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 4 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
Former unionist, broadcaster and urban Māori leader. Alliance MP 1999–2002 and Labour MP since 2017. Minister for Māori Development in the Labour government. Leads Labour's campaign for the Māori electorates.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Critical of Te Pāti Māori's overhang and candidate-vote strategy.
  • Concedes that unseating Māori-seat incumbents is hard.
