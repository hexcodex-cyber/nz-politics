Winston Peters

NZ First: Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “There is no such thing as the National government under MMP, doesn't he understand that?”

    5 November 2026 · Responding to Luxon ruling NZ First candidate Michael Laws out as a minister. · RNZ

  2. “It just has a whole lot of elitist Māori at the trough in which the mass Māori, the majority of Māori in whose name they make these demands, get absolutely nothing.”

    30 October 2026 · Interview with Whakaata Māori's Mata with Mihingarangi, on Te Aka Whaiora, as reported by RNZ on 30 Oct (interview date not stated). · RNZ

  3. “We've got to export, export, export, as we always have to.”

    23 October 2026 · Announcing NZ First's 50% accelerated depreciation policy at Pact Packaging, East Tāmaki. · interest.co.nz

  4. “We're going to make sure the people that concoct them are no longer wasting people's time.”

    20 October 2026 · Reacting to the 1News–Verian poll, which he called 'crap', and saying NZ First would 'do something about the TV1 polls' after the election. · 1News

  5. “In the first week or two, and for quite some time, the apprentice is not of great help to the enterprise. But they're learning fast, and businesses don't mind that, because they're on a slightly lesser wage while they learn. They're on about 80 percent That's the way it's always been.”

    17 October 2026 · On the Greens' plan to abolish the training wage. Asked whether that was enough to live on, he said many apprentices lived at home with support from their parents. · RNZ

  6. “It's a fantastic idea until you make it coercive and force it down people's throats ... If I'm getting on a train downtown and I'm in a hurry and I don't speak Māori, I'm not going to know where to go to because they decided to change all the names downtown.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, on compulsory te reo Māori and Māori station names. · 1News

  7. “New Zealand First will substantially reduce non-essential immigration and restore the principle that immigration is a privilege, not a right.”

    11 September 2026 · Unveiling NZ First's immigration platform, including a referendum on capping resident visas at 20,000 a year. · RNZ

  8. “It was their deal, the ACT Party's deal with the National Party. We were opposed to this from the word go but you've only got so many cards you can play.”

    20 November 2025 · Vowing to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act a week after all eight NZ First MPs voted for its third reading. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • All eight NZ First MPs voted for the Regulatory Standards Act at third reading (13 November 2025). On 20 November he vowed to repeal it, saying NZ First had always opposed it but was bound by ACT's deal with National. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 1 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
In Parliament on and off since 1978. Founded NZ First in 1993. Three times Deputy PM, most recently from November 2023 to 31 May 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Cut 'non-essential' immigration (20,000 resident visa cap by referendum). Abolish the Waitangi Tribunal.
  • Repeal the Regulatory Standards Act. Hostile to the Opportunity Party and the 'mainstream media'.
