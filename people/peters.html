Winston Peters

NZ First: Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “There is no substitute for diesel for industry. Likewise, ordinary consumers have suffered too much with the cost of petrol.”

    24 October 2026 · Public meeting in Thames, announcing NZ First's plan to remove the ETS charge from fuel (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  2. “Those countries are equal with us. We don't talk down. We don't look down to them. We treat them as equal.”

    22 October 2026 · On criticism of the Pacific Islands Forum's decision to make Israel a partner (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  3. “You've got members of Parliament saying the gangs are justified in their behaviour, because they're the victim of what? Colonisation? I've never heard such a disgraceful statement made.”

    20 October 2026 · Speaking to media in Auckland about political tension and safety (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “In the first week or two, and for quite some time, the apprentice is not of great help to the enterprise. But they're learning fast, and businesses don't mind that, because they're on a slightly lesser wage while they learn. They're on about 80 percent That's the way it's always been.”

    17 October 2026 · On the Greens' plan to abolish the training wage. Asked whether that was enough to live on, he said many apprentices lived at home with support from their parents. · RNZ

  5. “We can afford it, but we can't afford it with them because they can't run a sound economy. We in New Zealand First can.”

    13 October 2026 · On whether NZ Super is affordable, responding to National's fiscal plan (reported by RNZ) · RNZ

  6. “They're the mainstream media's party, and you guys are no good at politics.”

    6 October 2026 · Predicting the Opportunity Party would fall short of 5%, after a 1News–Verian poll put it on 7%. · 1News

  7. “We need immigration to New Zealand. We always have. It has been an essential part of our history...”

    11 September 2026 · Same announcement. · RNZ

  8. “It was their deal, the ACT Party's deal with the National Party. We were opposed to this from the word go but you've only got so many cards you can play.”

    20 November 2025 · Vowing to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act a week after all eight NZ First MPs voted for its third reading. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • All eight NZ First MPs voted for the Regulatory Standards Act at third reading (13 November 2025). On 20 November he vowed to repeal it, saying NZ First had always opposed it but was bound by ACT's deal with National. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 1 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
In Parliament on and off since 1978. Founded NZ First in 1993. Three times Deputy PM, most recently from November 2023 to 31 May 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Cut 'non-essential' immigration (20,000 resident visa cap by referendum). Abolish the Waitangi Tribunal.
  • Repeal the Regulatory Standards Act. Hostile to the Opportunity Party and the 'mainstream media'.
