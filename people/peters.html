Winston Peters

NZ First: Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We believe that the child should have a chance to grow up and make a decision. Not have a decision made for it and regret it for the rest of his life.”

    22 October 2026 · After the High Court set aside the government's puberty-blocker restrictions, saying the ruling should be appealed (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  2. “No, no ... as a child, I got a few smackings and guess what? I thoroughly deserved them. Make no bones about it.”

    21 October 2026 · NZ Herald Powerbrokers' debate, asked about NZ First's position on the anti-smacking law. · NZ Herald

  3. “We are not proposing to spend years and millions of dollars studying whether Cantabrians might catch a train. We are proposing to put the trains on and find out.”

    18 October 2026 · Announcing in Christchurch NZ First's 12-month commuter rail trial for Canterbury (RNZ). · RNZ

  4. “We are a nationalist party who puts New Zealand and New Zealanders first.”

    16 October 2026 · Closing line of the same aluminium post. · X post

  5. “It's a fantastic idea until you make it coercive and force it down people's throats ... If I'm getting on a train downtown and I'm in a hurry and I don't speak Māori, I'm not going to know where to go to because they decided to change all the names downtown.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, on compulsory te reo Māori and Māori station names. · 1News

  6. “They're the mainstream media's party, and you guys are no good at politics.”

    6 October 2026 · Predicting the Opportunity Party would fall short of 5%, after a 1News–Verian poll put it on 7%. · 1News

  7. “After days of speculation and distraction it has boiled down to 'much ado about nothing'.”

    12 August 2026 · On Luxon surviving the Penk leadership challenge. · 1News

  8. “It was their deal, the ACT Party's deal with the National Party. We were opposed to this from the word go but you've only got so many cards you can play.”

    20 November 2025 · Vowing to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act a week after all eight NZ First MPs voted for its third reading. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • All eight NZ First MPs voted for the Regulatory Standards Act at third reading (13 November 2025). On 20 November he vowed to repeal it, saying NZ First had always opposed it but was bound by ACT's deal with National. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 1 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
In Parliament on and off since 1978. Founded NZ First in 1993. Three times Deputy PM, most recently from November 2023 to 31 May 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Cut 'non-essential' immigration (20,000 resident visa cap by referendum). Abolish the Waitangi Tribunal.
  • Repeal the Regulatory Standards Act. Hostile to the Opportunity Party and the 'mainstream media'.
