Winston Peters

NZ First: Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We're going to make sure the people that concoct them are no longer wasting people's time.”

    20 October 2026 · Reacting to the 1News–Verian poll, which he called 'crap', and saying NZ First would 'do something about the TV1 polls' after the election. · 1News

  2. “We are not proposing to spend years and millions of dollars studying whether Cantabrians might catch a train. We are proposing to put the trains on and find out.”

    18 October 2026 · Announcing in Christchurch NZ First's 12-month commuter rail trial for Canterbury (RNZ). · RNZ

  3. “NZFirst will immediately reverse this week’s decision by the Minister of Consumer Affairs before the end of this year.”

    16 October 2026 · X post after visiting Vulcan Aluminium in Hamilton, on the decision not to impose a safeguard on imported aluminium extrusions. · X post

  4. “We can afford it, but we can't afford it with them because they can't run a sound economy. We in New Zealand First can.”

    13 October 2026 · On whether NZ Super is affordable, responding to National's fiscal plan (reported by RNZ) · RNZ

  5. “I've turned everything down — knighthoods and embassies and high commissions and being a diplomat and what have you.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, asked about becoming New Zealand's longest-serving MP if re-elected. · 1News

  6. “The Treaty of Waitangi Tribunal has become a make-work organisation – now hearing any and all claims that in a former time would've been dismissed on application as being inappropriate.”

    27 September 2026 · X post after announcing NZ First would abolish the Waitangi Tribunal within 100 days of the next government. · X post

  7. “After days of speculation and distraction it has boiled down to 'much ado about nothing'.”

    12 August 2026 · On Luxon surviving the Penk leadership challenge. · 1News

  8. “It was their deal, the ACT Party's deal with the National Party. We were opposed to this from the word go but you've only got so many cards you can play.”

    20 November 2025 · Vowing to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act a week after all eight NZ First MPs voted for its third reading. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • All eight NZ First MPs voted for the Regulatory Standards Act at third reading (13 November 2025). On 20 November he vowed to repeal it, saying NZ First had always opposed it but was bound by ACT's deal with National. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 1 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
In Parliament on and off since 1978. Founded NZ First in 1993. Three times Deputy PM, most recently from November 2023 to 31 May 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Cut 'non-essential' immigration (20,000 resident visa cap by referendum). Abolish the Waitangi Tribunal.
  • Repeal the Regulatory Standards Act. Hostile to the Opportunity Party and the 'mainstream media'.
