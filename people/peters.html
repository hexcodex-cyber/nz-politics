Winston Peters

NZ First: Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We did release our plan. Guess what it's called? It's a collective plan we got together called the Budget 2026. And there are refinements we'll give you in this election campaign, and in every case we've given you the costings.”

    17 October 2026 · Defending NZ First's decision not to release a separate fiscal plan; Labour, National and ACT have released theirs and the Greens' is due next week (RNZ live blog). · RNZ

  2. “We are a nationalist party who puts New Zealand and New Zealanders first.”

    16 October 2026 · Closing line of the same aluminium post. · X post

  3. “We can afford it, but we can't afford it with them because they can't run a sound economy. We in New Zealand First can.”

    13 October 2026 · On whether NZ Super is affordable, responding to National's fiscal plan (reported by RNZ) · RNZ

  4. “I've turned everything down — knighthoods and embassies and high commissions and being a diplomat and what have you.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, asked about becoming New Zealand's longest-serving MP if re-elected. · 1News

  5. “They're the mainstream media's party, and you guys are no good at politics.”

    6 October 2026 · Predicting the Opportunity Party would fall short of 5%, after a 1News–Verian poll put it on 7%. · 1News

  6. “New Zealand First will substantially reduce non-essential immigration and restore the principle that immigration is a privilege, not a right.”

    11 September 2026 · Unveiling NZ First's immigration platform, including a referendum on capping resident visas at 20,000 a year. · RNZ

  7. “I'm defending our democracy, and I'm proud of it.”

    30 July 2026 · Ahead of Question Time, rejecting accusations of racism over the Xu-Nan remark (which he called "drivel") · 1News

  8. “It was their deal, the ACT Party's deal with the National Party. We were opposed to this from the word go but you've only got so many cards you can play.”

    20 November 2025 · Vowing to repeal the Regulatory Standards Act a week after all eight NZ First MPs voted for its third reading. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • All eight NZ First MPs voted for the Regulatory Standards Act at third reading (13 November 2025). On 20 November he vowed to repeal it, saying NZ First had always opposed it but was bound by ACT's deal with National. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of NZ First; Minister of Foreign Affairs; Racing; Rail
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 1 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
In Parliament on and off since 1978. Founded NZ First in 1993. Three times Deputy PM, most recently from November 2023 to 31 May 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Cut 'non-essential' immigration (20,000 resident visa cap by referendum). Abolish the Waitangi Tribunal.
  • Repeal the Regulatory Standards Act. Hostile to the Opportunity Party and the 'mainstream media'.
