What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The most expensive road ever built in 26 years is for two kilometres of road and adds one car lane in each direction and six to eight traffic lights.”

    15 October 2026 · Aro Valley candidates' debate (Wellington North, Thursday evening), on the planned State Highway 1 upgrade in Wellington. · RNZ

  2. “I'm not saying they should not exist altogether.”

    8 October 2026 · After saying being a billionaire was immoral (paraphrased in the same report); arguing extreme wealth should pay a fair share of tax. Date is the Midday Report interview day. · Pacific Media Network

  3. “This is the worst homelessness in our history, with 57,000 women homeless and half of all homeless people under the age of 24. The Government's response has been a drop in the bucket to meaningfully address this crisis.”

    18 June 2026 · Green release as housing spokesperson. · Green Party

  4. “If they comply and go home, they're not going to be charged. The minister realises they don't have a home, right? Where exactly are they supposed to move on? Should they go to your house?”

  5. “Where exactly are these kids meant to go? Would you like them to go sleep in a bush? Would you like them to go sleep under a bridge?”

    21 May 2026 · Same debate, objecting that the orders (as introduced) applied to people as young as 14. The final law applies to those 18 and over. · RNZ
