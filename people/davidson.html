Marama Davidson

Greens: Green Party co-leader

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “There is a responsibility for all of us in politics to understand the power and the influence of our platform.”

    23 October 2026 · Interview with Mata with Mihingarangi Forbes, on the attack on Australian MP Helen Haines (published by RNZ). · RNZ

  2. “Oriini Kaipara made those comments, not the Green Party, and that is for Te Pāti Māori to answer to.”

    19 October 2026 · Asked whether the Greens also wanted to 'restore mana' to the Mongrel Mob (RNZ). · RNZ

  3. “If we want people to enter trades and complete apprenticeships, it's time we fairly recognise their hard work instead of punishing them for trying to contribute.”

    17 October 2026 · Same announcement, at a building site in Auckland. · RNZ

  4. “We don't change who we are as a political party just for the campaign, you know.”

    15 October 2026 · RNZ interview on Thursday morning, standing by Green candidates' attendance at a Mongrel Mob election hui after Hipkins ruled Greens and Te Pāti Māori MPs out of police and corrections portfolios. · RNZ

  5. “We are unapologetic about making sure that we can engage with all communities, especially communities who are living on the margins. So we're proud, and we have done that year and year again.”

    14 October 2026 · Defending Green candidates' attendance at a Mongrel Mob whānau health and wellbeing election hui, after National criticism. · RNZ

  6. “Essential services like healthcare, education, and infrastructure could be funded if the super-rich and mega-corporates like the supermarkets duopoly, banks, and power companies contribute fairly to the society they profit from.”

    21 June 2026 · Greens' tax package launch. · RNZ

  7. “What is happening in the Middle East is, first and foremost, a human catastrophe. Civilians are being killed and injured, livelihoods are being destroyed, international law is being broken.”

    19 April 2026 · State of the Planet address, on the 2026 Iran war. · RNZ

  8. “We believe in building an international rules-based order that protects the environment, upholds human rights and supports enduring peace-building work.”

    19 April 2026 · Same address, on foreign policy. · RNZ

None noted in our sources.
