  1. “It will ensure collective bargaining power and strength to make sure that you have the work pay conditions and protections that you will deserve.”

    29 October 2026 · Workers First Union meeting in Auckland, on the Greens' automatic union membership policy. · RNZ

    23 October 2026 · Interview with Mata with Mihingarangi Forbes, on the attack on Australian MP Helen Haines (published by RNZ). · RNZ
