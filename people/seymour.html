David Seymour

ACT: Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We'll be happy with whatever result the voters give us, but we ask them to give their party vote ACT to re-elect the coalition, to make it go further and faster.”

    3 November 2026 · Reacting to the final 1News–Verian poll, which had ACT down two points to 10%. · 1News

  2. “Those MPs count just as much as extra National MPs. It's the size of the coalition that matters. So there's no risk to putting your party vote with ACT, you're still helping re-elect the coalition.”

    29 October 2026 · Public meeting at West Melton, Canterbury, telling supporters not to vote National to 'play it safe'. · RNZ

  3. “He doesn't know who he's talking to. I mean, he's got Chloe Swarbrick he's got to negotiate with. She's never been in government. Her party's never been in Cabinet. They don't know what they're doing.”

    26 October 2026 · St Heliers, responding to Hipkins' promise of a government by Christmas (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “It is a choice between financial security or financial ruin.”

    23 October 2026 · Responding to the Greens' fiscal plan. · RNZ

  5. “There's no limit to what you can achieve if you don't care who takes the credit.”

    21 October 2026 · After the Powerbrokers' debate, asked what advice he had for Luxon and Hipkins on reaching cross-party agreement. · RNZ

  6. “Maybe you don’t like Indian migrants or are concerned about migrants, but that’s not a reason to oppose the India FTA.”

    15 October 2026 · Same Wānaka meeting, answering a question about immigration and the India free trade agreement, which he called a 'red herring' for immigration concerns. · NZ Herald

  7. “You don't get higher wages from the government passing a law. You get it from risk taking and investment and innovation, people upskilling so they can produce more and get paid more.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, defending ACT's preference to freeze the minimum wage. · 1News

  8. “ACT will finish the settlements and wind up the Waitangi Tribunal. The Waitangi Tribunal has been part of New Zealand's constitutional landscape for more than fifty years. It has made an important contribution, particularly in helping address historical grievances.”

    10 September 2026 · X post announcing ACT's policy to dissolve the Tribunal once historical claims are settled (post continues). · X post

Profile

Role
Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation
Electorate / list
MP for Epsom (since 2014); contesting Epsom
Background
ACT leader since 2014 and the sponsor of the End of Life Choice Act. Led the Treaty Principles Bill (defeated in 2025) and the Regulatory Standards Act (passed November 2025).

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wind up the Waitangi Tribunal once settlements are finished.
  • Welfare obligations, including drug testing. Says 'both votes belong to the voter' on vote-splitting.
