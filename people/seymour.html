David Seymour

ACT: Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The government barely survived, it must now put its best foot forward in every step.”

    9 November 2026 · After the election, telling Stuff that National must recognise the voters' message and let its coalition partners play a bigger role, and expressing interest in the finance portfolio. Quoted here from the BBC's report of those remarks. · BBC

  2. “A party vote for ACT will not let Labour come through the middle. A party vote for ACT adds to the coalition just as effectively as a party vote for National.”

    31 October 2026 · Responding to Luxon's disappointment over the overhang ad. · RNZ

  3. “Every other political party, even National, wants to spend more of your money. ACT alone is proposing to save your money.”

    26 October 2026 · Written in ACT's manifesto, launched in Auckland. · RNZ

  4. “Instead of just learning about investment in a textbook, students will have real skin in the game.”

    24 October 2026 · Announcing ACT's $500 investment account for every Year 11 student. · ACT press release

  5. “Epsom has always had a strategy, but Epsom has never been only a strategy. You do actually have to want the job, and do the job, and be up there representing people day by day, whether you think it's to your electoral advantage or not.”

    22 October 2026 · Epsom candidates' debate, replying to Paul Goldsmith's two-ticks pitch. · RNZ

  6. “The pension is linked to the wages. The only way we can help pensions in the real world is to raise wages through a stronger economy, get inflation under control, so things cost you less.”

    17 October 2026 · Answering a pensioner's question about support for disabled pensioners at the Warkworth Kōwhai Festival (RNZ live blog). · RNZ

  7. “You don't get higher wages from the government passing a law. You get it from risk taking and investment and innovation, people upskilling so they can produce more and get paid more.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, defending ACT's preference to freeze the minimum wage. · 1News

  8. “ACT will finish the settlements and wind up the Waitangi Tribunal. The Waitangi Tribunal has been part of New Zealand's constitutional landscape for more than fifty years. It has made an important contribution, particularly in helping address historical grievances.”

    10 September 2026 · X post announcing ACT's policy to dissolve the Tribunal once historical claims are settled (post continues). · X post

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation
Electorate / list
MP for Epsom (since 2014); contesting Epsom
Background
ACT leader since 2014 and the sponsor of the End of Life Choice Act. Led the Treaty Principles Bill (defeated in 2025) and the Regulatory Standards Act (passed November 2025).

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wind up the Waitangi Tribunal once settlements are finished.
  • Welfare obligations, including drug testing. Says 'both votes belong to the voter' on vote-splitting.
