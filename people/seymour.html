ACT: Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation

Profile

Role
Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation
Electorate / list
MP for Epsom (since 2014); contesting Epsom
Background
ACT leader since 2014 and the sponsor of the End of Life Choice Act. Led the Treaty Principles Bill (defeated in 2025) and the Regulatory Standards Act (passed November 2025).

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wind up the Waitangi Tribunal once settlements are finished.
  • Welfare obligations, including drug testing. Says 'both votes belong to the voter' on vote-splitting.
