David Seymour

ACT: Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “I do think that Chlöe would be bad, but the issue is not whether Chlöe's finance minister. The issue is whether our coalition is re-elected. That's what we're fighting for.”

    31 October 2026 · Newmarket, Auckland, asked about Swarbrick as finance minister. · RNZ live blog

  2. “Yes, I am, because you seem to be saying that there's absolutely no agency that a person has. Actually, people can choose.”

    27 October 2026 · RNZ First Up, asked whether he stood by the view that people use a 'sob story' to excuse crime, amid claims linking abuse in state care to gangs (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  3. “If you take all the benefits of a free and democratic society, but you're not prepared to perform a task simpler than ordering a pizza on Uber Eats to be part of that society, I think that is dropkick behaviour.”

    25 October 2026 · On people who do not enrol, on the final day to enrol. · RNZ

  4. “I think she has revealed that what's good for the goose is good for the gangster.”

    23 October 2026 · Daily media conference, on Kerrin Leoni's resignation (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “it's essential we don't have a change of government at this time and it's also essential, in my view, that the new coalition has more ACT in it so we can actually grapple with these challenges”

    20 October 2026 · RNZ First Up interview; he said ACT's job was to ensure the coalition is re-elected (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  6. “Maybe you don’t like Indian migrants or are concerned about migrants, but that’s not a reason to oppose the India FTA.”

    15 October 2026 · Same Wānaka meeting, answering a question about immigration and the India free trade agreement, which he called a 'red herring' for immigration concerns. · NZ Herald

  7. “Now, there's one of two failings: either New Zealand has become a much sicker place in the last seven years, or we have got a lot worse at managing and helping people who could be working. And I suspect it's the latter.”

    12 October 2026 · Defending ACT's fiscal-plan proposal to cut disability benefit spending through independent medical certification · RNZ

  8. “ACT will finish the settlements and wind up the Waitangi Tribunal. The Waitangi Tribunal has been part of New Zealand's constitutional landscape for more than fifty years. It has made an important contribution, particularly in helping address historical grievances.”

    10 September 2026 · X post announcing ACT's policy to dissolve the Tribunal once historical claims are settled (post continues). · X post

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation
Electorate / list
MP for Epsom (since 2014); contesting Epsom
Background
ACT leader since 2014 and the sponsor of the End of Life Choice Act. Led the Treaty Principles Bill (defeated in 2025) and the Regulatory Standards Act (passed November 2025).

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wind up the Waitangi Tribunal once settlements are finished.
  • Welfare obligations, including drug testing. Says 'both votes belong to the voter' on vote-splitting.
