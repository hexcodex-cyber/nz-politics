David Seymour

ACT: Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “it's essential we don't have a change of government at this time and it's also essential, in my view, that the new coalition has more ACT in it so we can actually grapple with these challenges”

    20 October 2026 · RNZ First Up interview; he said ACT's job was to ensure the coalition is re-elected (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  2. “You almost wonder if the Green Party is not campaigning for Australia, because that's where more young people will end up with under their policy.”

    17 October 2026 · On the Greens' training wage policy, which he said would make it $10,000 more expensive to take on an apprentice. ACT would cut the training wage to 60% of the minimum wage. · RNZ

  3. “some four-headed beast rather than [Green co-leaders] Chlöe [Swarbrick] and Marama [Davidson] running the country from the Mongrel Mob headquarters, then that’s probably a good outcome”

    15 October 2026 · Public meeting in Wānaka, asked how ACT would work with Opportunity in a right-leaning bloc. Fragment as reported by the NZ Herald; he added that he doubted Opportunity would make it into Parliament. National has ruled Opportunity out. · NZ Herald

  4. “If cashflow is tight, it's much harder to carry out essential repairs if the tax deduction is delayed for years.”

    14 October 2026 · Announcing ACT's policy to let landlords offset rental losses against other income. · RNZ

  5. “The implication that somehow a New Zealand doctor who is there to treat people in a New Zealand cancer hospital is going to treat it like the Hunger Games is either ignorant or offensive.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to Swarbrick's Hunger Games remark. · 1News

  6. “If you're on a benefit you have an obligation to those paying taxes to fund it. You must be work ready, like they are. Many New Zealanders face random drug testing as a condition of work, so should the beneficiaries they fund.”

    8 October 2026 · X post backing drug testing of beneficiaries after National's welfare policy release. · X post

  7. “In my experience, voters are often busy, but they're not stupid, and it's never wise to talk down to them.”

    28 September 2026 · Responding to Luxon calling strategic vote-splitting "insanely stupid"; he said "both votes belong to the voter". · RNZ

  8. “ACT will finish the settlements and wind up the Waitangi Tribunal. The Waitangi Tribunal has been part of New Zealand's constitutional landscape for more than fifty years. It has made an important contribution, particularly in helping address historical grievances.”

    10 September 2026 · X post announcing ACT's policy to dissolve the Tribunal once historical claims are settled (post continues). · X post

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Leader of ACT; Deputy Prime Minister (since 31 May 2025); Minister for Regulation
Electorate / list
MP for Epsom (since 2014); contesting Epsom
Background
ACT leader since 2014 and the sponsor of the End of Life Choice Act. Led the Treaty Principles Bill (defeated in 2025) and the Regulatory Standards Act (passed November 2025).

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wind up the Waitangi Tribunal once settlements are finished.
  • Welfare obligations, including drug testing. Says 'both votes belong to the voter' on vote-splitting.
