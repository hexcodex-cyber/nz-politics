Nicola Willis

National: Deputy Leader of the National Party; Minister of Finance; Minister for Economic Growth; Minister for Social Investment

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “This is the least credible fiscal plan released by a major party in living memory.”

    4 October 2026 · National press release titled 'Labour's fiscal plan a fraud'. Labour says its plan is fully costed and funded. · National Party

  2. “I have looked at what the banks are being taxed at now and measures for changing that, and there are no other measures that I can see won't result in additional costs for Kiwis.”

    23 August 2026 · Explaining why National dropped a bank tax under its 'no new taxes' pledge. · 1News

  3. “It's a Budget that does what's right for the country... this is not a time to be getting us further into the hock, to be whacking another set of bills on the credit card.”

    29 May 2026 · RNZ Morning Report, asked whether a restrained Budget was risky in election year. · RNZ

  4. “I will go to the campaign trail as the National Party finance spokesperson with my view on what would be appropriate, well signalled, fair, transparent, gradual, gradual changes to New Zealand superannuation.”

    29 May 2026 · Post-Budget, on NZ Super costs rising; she added: "I haven't been able to convince my coalition partners yet, but I retain hope." · RNZ

  5. “New Zealand is digging its way out of the post-Covid hole.”

    28 May 2026 · Delivering Budget 2026. · RNZ

  6. “Kiwis can look forward with confidence, they just need to choose it, they just need to vote for it.”

    28 May 2026 · Closing line of her Budget 2026 speech. · RNZ

  7. “We've got to make sure the banks are taxed fairly... As a minister in this Government, I am still actively considering the taxation of banks and what that looks like.”

    May 2026 · May 2026 comments (exact day not given in source), as quoted by 1News when she later dropped the idea. · 1News

  8. “One for all, all for one. And when the caucus, by majority, have confidence in the leader, then we all stand together, backing the leader.”

    21 April 2026 · As deputy leader, after Luxon survived the April caucus confidence vote. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • In May 2026 she said 'We've got to make sure the banks are taxed fairly…'. On 23 August 2026 she explained why National would not pursue a bank tax. [1News]

Profile

Role
Deputy Leader of the National Party; Minister of Finance; Minister for Economic Growth; Minister for Social Investment
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 2 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
Entered Parliament on the list in 2018 after a career as a political adviser and corporate lobbyist. Deputy leader since December 2021; Finance Minister since 2023; delivered Budget 2026 on 28 May.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Fiscal restraint in an election year. Calls Labour's fiscal plan 'the least credible… in living memory'.
  • Personally favours raising the NZ Super age, but says she has not convinced coalition partners.
  • Floated taxing banks 'fairly' in May 2026, then dropped it under the 'no new taxes' pledge.
