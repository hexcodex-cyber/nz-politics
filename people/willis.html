Nicola Willis

National: Deputy Leader of the National Party; Minister of Finance; Minister for Economic Growth; Minister for Social Investment

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We have been fixing the underlying foundations, and New Zealand will be seeing the rewards if we stick to the plan.”

    22 October 2026 · Media stand-up on the September-quarter inflation figures (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  2. “I'm really interested in the R&D tax credit, which has been successful in encouraging firms to invest in innovation, and there's potential for expanding that. Reversals or contradictions

  • In May 2026 she said 'We've got to make sure the banks are taxed fairly…'. On 23 August 2026 she explained why National would not pursue a bank tax. [1News]

Profile

Role
Deputy Leader of the National Party; Minister of Finance; Minister for Economic Growth; Minister for Social Investment
Electorate / list
List MP; list rank 2 (not contesting an electorate)
Background
Entered Parliament on the list in 2018 after a career as a political adviser and corporate lobbyist. Deputy leader since December 2021; Finance Minister since 2023; delivered Budget 2026 on 28 May.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Fiscal restraint in an election year. Calls Labour's fiscal plan 'the least credible… in living memory'.
  • Personally favours raising the NZ Super age gradually. National's 2026 policy is cross-party talks within 100 days rather than a set date.
  • Tax relief only once the books are back in surplus (not forecast before 2029). On 14 October she signalled interest in future business tax relief (including the R&D tax credit and, over time, the corporate rate) alongside personal tax cuts when conditions allow.
  • Floated taxing banks 'fairly' in May 2026, then dropped it under the 'no new taxes' pledge.
