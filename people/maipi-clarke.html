Hana-Rāwhiti Maipi-Clarke

Te Pāti Māori: MP; youngest member of the 54th Parliament

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Iwi and hapuu voices matter. Decisions about our whenua and communities must be made with the people who whakapapa to those places.”

    24 August 2026 · Statement on Waikato and Waipā river commitments · Te Ao Māori News

  2. “We call on the Government to honour its commitments and enter into a new era of co-management and co-governance with Waikato-Tainui, as set out in the Waikato-Tainui Raupatu Claims (Waikato River) Settlement Act 2010.”

    24 August 2026 · Same statement · Te Ao Māori News

  3. “He was the reason why I was inspired to come into politics.”

    19 June 2026 · On co-leader Rawiri Waititi, TVNZ interview with Scotty Morrison confirming she is standing again for Te Pāti Māori · 1News

  4. “you're going up against the 115 people who are against you”

    19 June 2026 · Describing debating in Parliament, same TVNZ interview · 1News

  5. “I think you have this really naive energy engine room in your soul when you're so young, you think you can solve all the world's problems, especially when you've got a grandfather and grandparents like mine, and you just use that energy while you can.”

    19 June 2026 · Same TVNZ interview · 1News

Profile

Role
MP; youngest member of the 54th Parliament
Electorate / list
MP for Hauraki-Waikato (since 2023); contesting Hauraki-Waikato
Background
Defeated Nanaia Mahuta in 2023. Her maiden-speech haka and the 2024 Treaty Principles Bill haka drew global attention. Suspended for 7 days in 2025.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Wants Waikato River co-management and co-governance honoured. Criticises 'tribalism' in politics.
