John Tamihere

Te Pāti Māori: President of Te Pāti Māori (not an MP)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “It's clear to our people that if they want an unapologetic voice, if they want more Māori in parliament, and they want to get this government out, this is the strategy.”

    5 October 2026 · On the candidate-vote-only strategy · The Spinoff

  2. “It's not untoward where political parties have dissension and difficulty. It's a matter of growing through that, and it's where we are at the moment.”

    1 May 2026 · Interview with Te Ao Māori News on party turmoil · Te Ao Māori News

  3. “Debbie and Rawiri built the brand out of nothing between 2020 and 2023.”

    1 May 2026 · Same interview, on the co-leaders · Te Ao Māori News

  4. “You can't have a change of government unless Te Pāti Māori's MPs are there. This is just the mathematical fact.”

    1 May 2026 · Same interview, on Labour and Green challenges in Māori seats · Te Ao Māori News

  5. “So a vote for Labour or a vote for the Greens is a vote for New Zealand First.”

    1 May 2026 · Same interview, immediately following the previous remark, in the context of Labour/Green candidates contesting Māori electorates · Te Ao Māori News

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

During the Kapa-Kingi court case, Kapa-Kingi alleged that Tamihere made threats. These are allegations reported by The Spinoff, not findings against him, and we have not quoted them here.

Profile

Role
President of Te Pāti Māori (not an MP)
Electorate / list
Not a candidate for Parliament in 2026 (not on list)
Background
Labour MP 1999–2005 (Cabinet minister 2002–04). Briefly TPM co-leader in 2020, and party president since June 2022, including during the 2025–26 expulsions.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Says a change of government is impossible without TPM MPs. Defends the candidate-vote strategy.
