Oriini Kaipara

Te Pāti Māori: MP

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We have been very open about our strategy which is the one candidate. We want one-tick, candidate vote, for the person that we've put up as Te Pati Māori in each of the seven Māori electorates.”

    9 October 2026 · Tāmaki Makaurau candidates' debate (Marae) · 1News

  2. “All our policies have been designed by Māori for Māori to Māori, and it really does give weight to, and rise to, the fact that what's good for Māori is good for all of Aotearoa.”

    9 October 2026 · Same debate, on the party's tax policy · 1News

  3. “There's no more emergency housing to catch those who are homeless at the moment, we're just moving them on, round and round in circles, creating more deprivation, punching down on the pōhara instead of creating real viable solutions.”

    9 October 2026 · Same debate, on housing · 1News

  4. “I confirm that the comments claiming to be mine are not. I did not and have not spoken with any reporter on this matter.”

    12 May 2026 · Social media post rejecting a Stuff report that she was 'considering options' outside the party · RNZ

  5. “committed member of Te Pāti Māori, committed to Tāmaki Makaurau, and committed to making this a one term government.”

    12 May 2026 · Same social media post · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
MP
Electorate / list
MP for Tāmaki Makaurau (since the September 2025 by-election); contesting Tāmaki Makaurau
Background
Former broadcaster, and the first newsreader with moko kauae on a mainstream New Zealand news bulletin. Won the by-election after the death of Takutai Tarsh Kemp.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Te Tiriti is 'absolutely' non-negotiable. Supports the tax-free first $30,000 and Māori-targeted public housing.
