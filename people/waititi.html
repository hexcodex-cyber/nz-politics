What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “There's nothing wrong with being bilingual ... I'm quite proficient, I would say, in the English language, but the majority of Aotearoa are not proficient in my language, so they only think in one world. I think in two.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, on compulsory te reo Māori in schools. · 1News

  2. “You poor thing.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to Peters' complaint that Māori City Rail Link station names are confusing. · 1News

  3. “This list proves we are putting our money where our mouth is.”

    4 October 2026 · On the party's one-person list · RNZ

  4. “We made a deliberate decision to put collective Māori representation ahead of building a long party list.”

    4 October 2026 · On the party's one-person list · RNZ

    16 August 2026 · Campaign launch, explaining the candidate-vote strategy using Te Tai Tokerau as an example · RNZ

  6. “suggestion is not ours to make”

    16 August 2026 · Asked by media which party Māori voters should give their party vote to · RNZ

Profile

Role
Co-leader of Te Pāti Māori
Electorate / list
MP for Waiariki (since 2020); contesting Waiariki
Background
Co-leader since 2020, after winning Waiariki from Labour. Suspended from Parliament for 21 days in 2025 over the Treaty Principles Bill haka.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Candidate-vote strategy aiming for an overhang. Refuses to tell voters where to put their party vote.
