Rawiri Waititi

Te Pāti Māori: Co-leader of Te Pāti Māori

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Our whānau do not stop being our whānau because they wear a patch, and our responsibility to them does not disappear because someone finds their presence politically inconvenient.”

    23 October 2026 · Te Pāti Māori statement after Kerrin Leoni's resignation. · RNZ

  2. “The right benefits every time we turn on each other, and we will not give them that victory.”

    23 October 2026 · Statement after Kerrin Leoni's resignation, distancing Te Pāti Māori from its role in her downfall (NZ Herald report via Newstalk ZB, 24 Oct). · Newstalk ZB / NZ Herald

  3. “You poor thing.”

    13 October 2026 · Multi-Party Debate, responding to Peters' complaint that Māori City Rail Link station names are confusing. · 1News

  4. “When our creativity is strong, our people are strong.”

    12 October 2026 · Te Pāti Māori press release announcing an independent Toi Māori Authority and an income programme for Māori artists, in Ōtautahi (via Scoop). · Te Pāti Māori release via Scoop

  5. “We made a deliberate decision to put collective Māori representation ahead of building a long party list.”

    4 October 2026 · On the party's one-person list · RNZ

  6. “You give your candidate vote to Aperehama, [and] Willow-Jean Prime - who is at seven on her party list - and Huhana Lyndon - who is also on seven on her party list - you get three... and so on and so forth, and that's how you work it out.”

    16 August 2026 · Campaign launch, explaining the candidate-vote strategy using Te Tai Tokerau as an example · RNZ

  7. “I can hear the anger and feel the pain.”

    9 February 2026 · Responding at Waitangi pōwhiri to Eru Kapa-Kingi's criticism of the party's expulsion of his mother (date shown is publication date of the report) · RNZ

  8. “If I have done you wrong, I offer you my head.”

    9 February 2026 · Same Waitangi pōwhiri, offering an apology and suggesting another meeting with Ngāpuhi (date shown is publication date of the report) · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Co-leader of Te Pāti Māori
Electorate / list
MP for Waiariki (since 2020); contesting Waiariki
Background
Co-leader since 2020, after winning Waiariki from Labour. Suspended from Parliament for 21 days in 2025 over the Treaty Principles Bill haka.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Candidate-vote strategy aiming for an overhang. Refuses to tell voters where to put their party vote.
