Andrew Hoggard

ACT: Minister for Biosecurity; Minister for Food Safety; Associate Agriculture

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Farmers deserve a massive thank you. While politicians in Wellington talk about prosperity, rural New Zealand goes out and earns it.”

    11 June 2026 · ACT release on record forecast primary-sector exports ($64.3b). · Scoop (ACT release)

  2. “Punishing them with ideological targets while overseas competitors produce food less efficiently is bad for farmers, bad for consumers, and bad for the climate.”

    11 June 2026 · Same release, on ACT's 'science-based' (split-gas) climate approach for agriculture. · Scoop (ACT release)

  3. “New Zealand's farms, orchards, and fishing fleets generate $60 billion in exports every year. Right now, they can't find enough capable workers to do the job, held back by a chronic labour shortage that the current immigration system simply isn't equipped to solve.”

    9 June 2026 · Announcing ACT's Rural Workforce Visa at Fieldays. · Scoop (ACT release)

  4. “While discussions are ongoing, Cabinet has not agreed any final decisions.”

    10 March 2026 · On the coalition's stalled pledge to reverse the live-animal-export ban, which he said he could not get Cabinet agreement on this term. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • The coalition (via the ACT agreement) promised to reverse the live-animal-export ban. In March 2026 Hoggard said Cabinet had 'not agreed any final decisions', and exporters called this a backtrack. [RNZ]
