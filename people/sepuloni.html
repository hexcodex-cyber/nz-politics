Carmel Sepuloni

Labour: Deputy Leader of the Labour Party

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We're talking about seamless travel between Pacific countries and New Zealand … and a recognition of the cost and burden that the current system puts on New Zealand Pacific families.”

    17 September 2026 · Announcing Labour's pledge to extend visa-waiver travel to Pacific Islands Forum citizens. · Asia Pacific Report

  2. “Our relationship has matured enough for us to be able to move to a place that they have access to the same electronic system that 60 other countries have. It's the right thing to do.”

    17 September 2026 · Same announcement. · Asia Pacific Report

  3. “The expectation is that the rules still apply … and our expectation is that people will follow them.”

    17 September 2026 · Responding to concerns about rising overstayer rates. · Asia Pacific Report

  4. “It's not nice, and it's hard when you have to explain it to your kids, but it's even worse for the people that are actually living as homeless people, because they have nowhere to lay down with a roof over their heads at night time.”

    21 May 2026 · First-reading debate on the move-on orders bill, which she called "purely ideological". · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Profile

Role
Deputy Leader of the Labour Party
Electorate / list
Electorate MP (Kelston); contesting the new Glendene electorate
Background
List MP 2008–11, MP for Kelston since 2014. Social Development Minister throughout the Ardern government, Deputy Prime Minister in 2023, and Labour deputy leader since 2023. First MP of Tongan descent.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Visa-waiver travel for Pacific Islands Forum citizens, while 'the rules still apply' on overstaying.
  • Opposed the move-on orders bill as 'purely ideological'.
