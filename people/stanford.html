Erica Stanford

National: Minister of Education; Minister of Immigration; lead minister on the Royal Commission (abuse in care) response

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “What I will not apologise for is raising Māori achievement, because that's what we're doing.”

    1 November 2026 · TVNZ Q+A, on the Waitangi Tribunal's finding over removing Treaty obligations from school boards. · 1News

  2. “It means that you have to get out there and party vote National. Don't get cute about it.”

    1 November 2026 · TVNZ Q+A, after citing National's internal polling, a week before election day. · 1News

  3. “Removing section 127(1)(d) does not remove school boards' responsibilities to Māori students.”

    30 October 2026 · Statement responding to a Waitangi Tribunal report calling for an apology over removing school boards' Treaty obligations. · RNZ

  4. “Kiwis and parents can see the transformation of our education system with these reforms. They can see it in their children, they know that it's working.”

    21 October 2026 · On the 1News–Verian finding that 70% back the hour-a-day reading, writing and maths rule. · 1News

  5. “Labour is asking New Zealanders to put them back in charge of education, but they can’t even agree among themselves what their education policy is.”

    14 October 2026 · National release after Hipkins said at the NZ Herald leaders' debate that the hour-a-day reading, writing and maths requirement should be left to schools, and Edmonds earlier said Labour would not change it. · National Party

  6. “I didn't challenge for the leadership.”

    16 August 2026 · First media appearance after the failed Penk leadership challenge, amid speculation she was the alternative leader. · RNZ

  7. “I didn't do any numbers at all. I can categorically tell you, I don't know if anyone did do any numbers. I certainly didn't.”

    16 August 2026 · Denying she counted caucus votes for Chris Penk's challenge. · RNZ

  8. “We want young people leaving school with qualifications that are clear, rigorous and widely understood by parents, employers, tertiary providers and students themselves.”

    15 May 2026 · National Party release on the NCEA replacement. · National Party

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Labour's Willie Jackson publicly said 'Nobody believes her' about her denial. That is his claim; it is recorded on his profile.
