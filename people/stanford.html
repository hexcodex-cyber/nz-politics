Erica Stanford

National: Minister of Education; Minister of Immigration; lead minister on the Royal Commission (abuse in care) response

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “I didn't challenge for the leadership.”

    16 August 2026 · First media appearance after the failed Penk leadership challenge, amid speculation she was the alternative leader. · RNZ

  2. “I want to be the minister of Education and Immigration - mostly education - for the next nine years. I love my job, and I've got so much more to do.”

    16 August 2026 · Same appearance, ruling herself out of leadership ambitions. · RNZ

  3. “I didn't do any numbers at all. I can categorically tell you, I don't know if anyone did do any numbers. I certainly didn't.”

    16 August 2026 · Denying she counted caucus votes for Chris Penk's challenge. · RNZ

  4. “This qualification rewards hard work and is designed to encourage young people to strive to do their very best, and ultimately to feel proud of what they achieved.”

    16 May 2026 · Confirming details of the qualification replacing NCEA (NZCE at Year 12, NZACE at Year 13). · RNZ

  5. “We want young people leaving school with qualifications that are clear, rigorous and widely understood by parents, employers, tertiary providers and students themselves.”

    15 May 2026 · National Party release on the NCEA replacement. · National Party

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.

Labour's Willie Jackson publicly said 'Nobody believes her' about her denial. That is his claim; it is recorded on his profile.

Profile

Electorate / list

Background
Elected in 2017. Leads the replacement of NCEA and the school curriculum changes. Widely named in media as a possible alternative leader during the August 2026 challenge, which she denied any part in.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Replacing NCEA with new qualifications ('clear, rigorous and widely understood').
  • Says she did not challenge for, or count numbers for, the leadership.
