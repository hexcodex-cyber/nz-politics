Daniel Eb

Opportunity: Deputy Leader of the Opportunity Party (since March 2026)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “If you guys are voting purely based on organics, I don't believe that we have the best policy platform for you.”

    8 October 2026 · Closing remark in Organics Aotearoa New Zealand's 'Vote Organic' webinar (5 Oct), as agriculture spokesperson. Reported by the organic-sector news site Organic Food Together, which links the full recording. · Organic Food Together

  2. “We think there's a world in which we can do both.”

    8 October 2026 · Same webinar, asked whether New Zealand can protect its GE-free export status while allowing gene technology. Opportunity opposes the current Gene Technology Bill (as reported by Organic Food Together). · Organic Food Together

  3. “I don't know if that's the job of politicians.”

    8 October 2026 · Same webinar, asked to commit to a higher verification exemption threshold for small organic growers; he said he was six weeks into the agriculture role (as reported by Organic Food Together). · Organic Food Together

  4. “After almost 10 years working towards a more just, resilient and nature-positive food system in Aotearoa New Zealand, it's become clear to me that our national-level settings need to change. That's why I'm proud to stand for Opportunity, a party that is ready to lead the transformative, system-change reforms we need now”

    20 March 2026 · Announced as deputy leader and Kaipara ki Mahurangi candidate · Opportunity release via Scoop

Reversals or contradictions

None noted in our sources.
