Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We're seeing a very high turnout at this election. Our challenge for the next three days is to sustain that right the way through to 7 o'clock on Saturday, and we're going to have our teams on the ground doing that, and that is going to mean that people are going to be surprised with the results they see on Saturday night.”

    4 November 2026 · At a media stand-up in Paekākāriki on the Kāpiti Coast. · The Post

  2. “David Seymour would be disastrous as a minister of finance for New Zealand. He'd make Ruth Richardson and Roger Douglas look moderate.”

    30 October 2026 · In Dunedin, after Seymour said he might seek the finance portfolio. · RNZ live blog

  3. “Kerrin Leoni is not a Labour Party Candidate. We obviously can't have her name removed from the ballot. The ballot papers as you've seen, have already been printed. Really, what she does from here is up to her, but she will not be a Labour MP and not be a part of the Labour team.”

    26 October 2026 · After voting at Silverstream School, responding to Leoni running as an independent (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “All of us navigate family situations in politics, and it's bloody hard.”

    23 October 2026 · Hawke's Bay A&P show, on Kerrin Leoni's resignation as a Labour candidate. · RNZ

  5. “If you won't listen to the doctors and the nurses who are telling you the health system is in crisis, then you are never going to be able to fix it.”

    20 October 2026 · Final Stuff leaders' debate, saying Luxon was 'in denial'. · RNZ

  6. “Under our government, we achieved record low unemployment despite some of the most challenging economic conditions any government in New Zealand has faced ... we saved people's jobs [and] we've now got a government that celebrates losing them”

    16 October 2026 · Labour rally in Kelston, West Auckland, on the evening of Thursday 15 October; RNZ report published early on 16 October. · RNZ

  7. “I've had three years to prepare. I am running on a platform that I believe in. I'm campaigning to make changes that I passionately believe in.”

    11 October 2026 · Otago Daily Times leader interview with political editor Mike Houlahan · Otago Daily Times

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
