Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “I don't want that in itself become a political football where parties trying to manipulate the electoral system to their own advantage.”

    1 November 2026 · On being open to constitutional reform to stop future overhangs (wording as published by RNZ). · RNZ

  2. “If you pay your rent and look after your home, you should have the security of knowing you can stay there.”

    28 October 2026 · Announcing Labour's renters policy, including a ban on no-cause evictions, in Christchurch. · RNZ

  3. “If we do not have at least enrolment equivalent to what we had at the last election, that will be an indictment on this Government, who have deliberately tried to disenfranchise people, particularly younger New Zealanders, who are less likely to vote for them.”

    25 October 2026 · On the final day to enrol. · RNZ

  4. “Our Māori MPs get held to a very different standard to others.”

    23 October 2026 · Media stand-up on the reaction to Leoni's resignation. · RNZ

  5. “Winston Peters regularly says outrageous things, desperate for attention. He's not contributing constructively to political debate in New Zealand.”

    20 October 2026 · Speaking to reporters outside North Shore Hospital, after Peters said he should be jailed over the Covid response (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  6. “You can’t demand better roads, better pipes, more housing and better services whilst squeezing the funding that’s available to pay for those things.”

    15 October 2026 · Speech to Local Government New Zealand's SuperLocal conference in Rotorua, where he said Labour would expand its proposed ratepayer assistance scheme (Local Democracy Reporting via 1News). · 1News

  7. “I've had three years to prepare. I am running on a platform that I believe in. I'm campaigning to make changes that I passionately believe in.”

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
