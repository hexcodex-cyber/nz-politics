Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
