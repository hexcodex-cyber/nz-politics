Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “For New Zealanders, that would mean a hangover strategy on the 8th November. They would wake up with a whopping hangover and a government they didn't vote for.”

    30 October 2026 · In Dunedin, on ACT and NZ First approving an ad promoting the overhang strategy. · RNZ live blog

  2. “It just shows the desperation on their side. They know that we're doing well in this campaign and we're going to win it.”

    27 October 2026 · Mt Roskill media conference, on the Sensible Sentencing Trust's 'Backing the gangs' ad (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  3. “He now needs to answer why he thinks National MPs should be held to a different standard to the standard he demands of others.”

    24 October 2026 · Queensgate Mall, Lower Hutt, on Luxon's defence of Tama Potaka. · RNZ

  4. “If you really want to be tough on gangs, fix drug addiction because that's where they're making all of their money.”

    22 October 2026 · Answering a question on Māori imprisonment at the Hawke's Bay Chamber of Commerce (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “I have no time for the gangs. They will not get any support from any government that I lead.”

    19 October 2026 · Rejecting Oriini Kaipara's promise to 'restore mana' to Mongrel Mob members; he also said Labour would not support changes to the gang patch ban (RNZ). · RNZ

  6. “If they feel the process is rushed and they don't have the chance to be heard, they're less likely to buy into the final result.”

    15 October 2026 · Speech to the LGNZ SuperLocal conference in Rotorua, on local government reform and the paused Head Start programme (Local Democracy Reporting, via RNZ). · RNZ

  7. “Internally, I was in favour of campaigning on a CGT in 2020. We didn't do that, obviously. We've always been clear that we will only make changes to the tax system that we have a public mandate for.”

    11 October 2026 · Answering an audience question at a Birkenhead campaign meeting, the day Labour announced locally made school lunches from 2028 · 1News

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
