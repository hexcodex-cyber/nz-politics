Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Christopher Luxon doesn't want to talk about it, but everyone else does. So today, I'm challenging Christopher Luxon: come and debate me on health.”

    19 October 2026 · In a social media video challenging Luxon to a debate on health, after Luxon denied the health system was in crisis (RNZ). · RNZ

  2. “The key thing we'd want to look at there is making sure that we're not doing anything that makes things worse for younger employees. And right at the moment, youth unemployment is a major issue. I want to leave no stone unturned in getting young people into jobs.”

    17 October 2026 · Asked in Auckland about the Greens' plan to abolish the training wage; he said Labour had not looked at it and wanted to review the evidence on employment effects. · RNZ

  3. “I think it was really, really ill considered, they made a real error of judgement here.”

    15 October 2026 · Speaking in Rotorua about Green and Te Pāti Māori candidates posing for photos at a Mongrel Mob election hui. He also said any of his potential ministers who did so 'wouldn't be a member of the cabinet'. · RNZ

  4. “[National] think women should settle for being paid less than men. I don't.”

    14 October 2026 · NZ Herald (NZME) leaders' debate with Chris Hipkins, 14 Oct evening, as reported in RNZ's analysis. Defending care and support workers after Luxon said Labour had not funded its pay equity promise. Bracketed word is RNZ's. · RNZ

  5. “We've got some real on-the-ground momentum everywhere we go ... People are raising the same issues with us. The cost of living is out of control. The current government's doing nothing about it. We can't afford three more years of this government.”

    13 October 2026 · Same poll reaction interview. · 1News

  6. “This election is about the cost of living. This election is about your cost of living.”

    27 September 2026 · Opening of his campaign launch speech in Manukau. · Labour Party (speech text)

  7. “Backstabbing and infighting just means more delay and more distraction for New Zealand. If National can't govern itself, how can New Zealanders trust it to govern the country?”

    12 August 2026 · Media release after Luxon survived the Penk leadership challenge. · 1News

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
