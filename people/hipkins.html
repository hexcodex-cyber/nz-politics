Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “All of us navigate family situations in politics, and it's bloody hard.”

    23 October 2026 · Hawke's Bay A&P show, on Kerrin Leoni's resignation as a Labour candidate. · RNZ

  2. “So I made a very clear line of saying: if something's underway, funded, let's keep it moving because there's no point of constantly changing our priorities all the time.”

    22 October 2026 · Hawke's Bay Chamber of Commerce, on not stopping infrastructure projects already underway (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  3. “Winston Peters regularly says outrageous things, desperate for attention. He's not contributing constructively to political debate in New Zealand.”

    20 October 2026 · Speaking to reporters outside North Shore Hospital, after Peters said he should be jailed over the Covid response (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  4. “The key thing we'd want to look at there is making sure that we're not doing anything that makes things worse for younger employees. And right at the moment, youth unemployment is a major issue. I want to leave no stone unturned in getting young people into jobs.”

    17 October 2026 · Asked in Auckland about the Greens' plan to abolish the training wage; he said Labour had not looked at it and wanted to review the evidence on employment effects. · RNZ

  5. “If they feel the process is rushed and they don't have the chance to be heard, they're less likely to buy into the final result.”

    15 October 2026 · Speech to the LGNZ SuperLocal conference in Rotorua, on local government reform and the paused Head Start programme (Local Democracy Reporting, via RNZ). · RNZ

  6. “We've got some real on-the-ground momentum everywhere we go ... People are raising the same issues with us. The cost of living is out of control. The current government's doing nothing about it. We can't afford three more years of this government.”

    13 October 2026 · Same poll reaction interview. · 1News

  7. “Neo-liberal, trickledown economics does not work.”

    27 September 2026 · Campaign launch, announcing a 10% write-off of eligible student loan balances. · RNZ

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
