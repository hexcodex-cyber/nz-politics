Chris Hipkins

Labour: Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “I don't want to get into the territory that we've seen overseas, where a party that doesn't do as well as they hoped in the election starts to scream about the illegitimacy of the elections. That's not a territory that I want to get into.”

    18 October 2026 · TVNZ Q+A interview with Jack Tame, asked whether his criticism of the enrolment-law changes risked undermining the election's legitimacy. He said Labour would accept the result (1News). · 1News

  2. “We will not be providing any funding to any types of gang programmes in the government.”

    17 October 2026 · Speaking to more than 100 ethnic community leaders at the Swaminarayan Temple in Papatoetoe, after Green and Te Pāti Māori candidates were photographed at a Mongrel Mob hui (RNZ live blog). · RNZ

  3. “If they feel the process is rushed and they don't have the chance to be heard, they're less likely to buy into the final result.”

    15 October 2026 · Speech to the LGNZ SuperLocal conference in Rotorua, on local government reform and the paused Head Start programme (Local Democracy Reporting, via RNZ). · RNZ

  4. “This is why people don't trust what you say.”

    14 October 2026 · NZ Herald (NZME) leaders' debate with Chris Hipkins, 14 Oct evening, as reported in RNZ's analysis. Accusing Luxon of lying after Luxon's warning that New Zealanders would 'pay' for Labour's capital gains tax. · RNZ

  5. “Cost should never be a barrier to a good education. We're focused on making life more affordable for families.”

    12 October 2026 · Announcing Labour's policy to cut school uniform costs · RNZ

  6. “This election is about the cost of living. This election is about your cost of living.”

    27 September 2026 · Opening of his campaign launch speech in Manukau. · Labour Party (speech text)

  7. “We want to send a really clear message that... you can work constructively with people, still have disagreements, without getting into abuse.”

    3 February 2026 · RNZ Morning Report, ahead of the joint Labour–Greens press conference at Waitangi. · Otago Daily Times (via RNZ)

  8. “#BREAKING: Labour will provide three free doctor's visits a year for everyone, delivered via a brand new Medicard and funded by a targeted tax on property profits, excluding the family home.”

    28 October 2025 · X post announcing Labour's flagship health/tax policy. · X post

Reversals or contradictions

  • In July 2023 he said: "under a government I lead there will be no wealth or capital gains tax after the election. End of story." In October 2025 Labour adopted a 28% CGT on property. After the 2023 loss he put tax options back on the table, and he says the CGT is narrow and targeted. [RNZ]
  • RNZ background on the 2025 decision and earlier ruling-out. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Leader of the Labour Party; Leader of the Opposition
Electorate / list
MP for Remutaka (since 2008); contesting Remutaka
Background
Was Education Minister and Covid-19 Response Minister in the Ardern government, and Prime Minister from January to November 2023. Has led Labour since January 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • Election framed as being 'about the cost of living'.
  • A property-only capital gains tax to fund three free GP visits a year; a 10% student-loan write-off.
  • Says talks with Opportunity are 'conversations to be had after the election'.
