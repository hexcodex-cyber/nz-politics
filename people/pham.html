Lan Pham

Greens: Environment spokesperson; list rank 6

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “With the passing of these bills, Aotearoa is in a new era of environmental exploitation at the hands of polluting industries.”

    23 September 2026 · Green release after the Planning Bill and Natural Environment Bill (RMA replacement) passed · Green Party release via newswire.nz

  2. “New Zealanders expect and deserve their laws to protect and restore the environment. Instead, the Luxon Government has chosen to act on industry demands and embed climate denialism and environmental destruction into planning and environmental law.”

    23 September 2026 · Same release · Green Party release via newswire.nz

  3. “on November 7, the Government will understand that nature may not have a voice, but voters certainly do.”

    23 September 2026 · Same release · Green Party release via newswire.nz

  5. “New Zealanders do not want mining companies let loose on our national parks and reserves. They expect conservation land to actually be conserved.”

    21 May 2026 · Same release · Green Party release
