Christopher Luxon

National: Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “The least someone can do is go get registered and enrolled to vote 12 days out from election day. It's not rocket science.”

    19 October 2026 · Told NewstalkZB, laughing off opposition claims of voter suppression over the end of same-day and advance-voting enrolment (RNZ live blog). · RNZ

  2. “Rural New Zealand produce the best food and fibre in the world. National has set an ambitious target to double exports by 2034, and we’re backing our farmers and growers to get there through more trade deals, better access to water and fertiliser, less red tape and improved land use rules.”

    16 October 2026 · X post on the day National released its agriculture manifesto at AgFest in Greymouth (opening of a longer post). · X post

  3. “It's really easy to burn a house down. Takes time to rebuild it.”

    14 October 2026 · NZ Herald (NZME) leaders' debate with Chris Hipkins, 14 Oct evening, as reported in RNZ's analysis. Asked why his government had yet to fix the economy. · RNZ

  4. “They were in the room where it happened, as Hamilton would say, and I'm not quite sure what they're banging on about today, because that is not quite right.”

    14 October 2026 · Responding to coalition partner NZ First's fast-track housing policy, which he called 'quite bizarre' because it was already law. · RNZ

  5. “If you can work, you should. Welfare should be firm, fair and affordable. A safety net for Kiwis who need it, not a system that gets abused at taxpayers' expense.”

    8 October 2026 · X post announcing National's Jobseeker changes, including random drug testing (posted 8 Oct NZT). · X post

  6. “I just wrapped up my campaign launch speech, and my message was simple: Where do you want your hard-earned money going, to you and your family, or to Labour and their mates? Party Vote National for no new taxes.”

    27 September 2026 · X post after National's campaign launch at the Auckland Convention Centre. · X post

  7. “Chris Penk launched a leadership challenge this week, which resulted in today's confidence vote. This makes his role untenable, and as such I have removed his portfolios.”

    12 August 2026 · Statement removing Chris Penk's ministerial portfolios. · 1News

  8. “We've been doing a big turnaround, we've been fixing a lot of the basics. We now have an opportunity, as this recovery comes through, not just to watch it come through but to actually shape the future of this country...”

    21 January 2026 · Election-date announcement; pitching National's economic record. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • National's tax pledge was originally 'no new taxes on working people'. In August 2026 Luxon widened it to 'no new taxes', dropping a bank tax and bed tax that ministers (including Willis) had floated earlier in the year. [1News]
  • In early May 2026 Luxon said National would 'go to the election with another election policy around superannuation and lifting the retirement age for sure'. Its 12 October fiscal plan set no date for raising the age and committed to cross-party talks instead. National says its stance has not changed. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence
Electorate / list
MP for Botany (since 2020); contesting Botany
Background
Former chief executive of Air New Zealand and a former Unilever executive. Entered Parliament in 2020, became National leader in November 2021 and Prime Minister in November 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • 'No new taxes' (no bank tax, no bed tax); tough welfare obligations, including drug testing for Jobseekers.
  • Has ruled out working with the Opportunity Party because of its land value tax; calls vote-splitting 'insanely stupid'.
  • Campaigns on economic 'turnaround' and 'fixing the basics'.
