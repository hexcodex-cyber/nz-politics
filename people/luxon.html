Christopher Luxon

National: Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

    13 October 2026 · Explaining why National's superannuation policy is cross-party talks rather than a set date for raising the age (reported by RNZ) · RNZ

  2. “We want to work with parties that want no new taxes. It's as simple as that.”

Reversals or contradictions

  • National's tax pledge was originally 'no new taxes on working people'. In August 2026 Luxon widened it to 'no new taxes', dropping a bank tax and bed tax that ministers (including Willis) had floated earlier in the year. [1News]
  • In early May 2026 Luxon said National would 'go to the election with another election policy around superannuation and lifting the retirement age for sure'. Its 12 October fiscal plan set no date for raising the age and committed to cross-party talks instead. National says its stance has not changed. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence
Electorate / list
MP for Botany (since 2020); contesting Botany
Background
Former chief executive of Air New Zealand and a former Unilever executive. Entered Parliament in 2020, became National leader in November 2021 and Prime Minister in November 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • 'No new taxes' (no bank tax, no bed tax); tough welfare obligations, including drug testing for Jobseekers.
  • Has ruled out working with the Opportunity Party because of its land value tax; calls vote-splitting 'insanely stupid'.
  • Campaigns on economic 'turnaround' and 'fixing the basics'.
