Christopher Luxon

National: Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “There is no plan B. We're all in.”

    5 November 2026 · On National's campaign bus, asked about his plans if not re-elected; he said he was loving the close campaign. · The Post

  2. “If RNZ's poll today becomes reality, there will be dire consequences for Kiwis.”

    29 October 2026 · Social media post responding to the RNZ–Reid Research poll (National 24.8%), despite usually saying he doesn't comment on polls. · RNZ

  3. “Turning around Labour's mess was never going to be a three-year job and National needs more time to finish it. Our 100 Day Plan sets out what we will prioritise if re-elected to build on our progress.”

    25 October 2026 · Releasing National's 100-day plan at a rally in Browns Bay, Auckland. · RNZ

  4. “I mean, the video didn't look like a family gathering. It looked like someone in Labour Party gear campaigning.”

    23 October 2026 · Hawke's Bay A&P show, asked whether Leoni was the victim of a pile-on (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “They are not Probus or the Lions or Rotary, they're not a community organisation, they peddle misery and death and suffering.”

    19 October 2026 · Responding to Oriini Kaipara's comments on the Mongrel Mob (RNZ). · RNZ

  6. “It's really easy to burn a house down. Takes time to rebuild it.”

    14 October 2026 · NZ Herald (NZME) leaders' debate with Chris Hipkins, 14 Oct evening, as reported in RNZ's analysis. Asked why his government had yet to fix the economy. · RNZ

  7. “It's absolute madness.”

    28 September 2026 · On RNZ Morning Report, responding to NZ First's policy of buying back BNZ (said 'that's just not going to happen'). · RNZ

  8. “We've been doing a big turnaround, we've been fixing a lot of the basics. We now have an opportunity, as this recovery comes through, not just to watch it come through but to actually shape the future of this country...”

    21 January 2026 · Election-date announcement; pitching National's economic record. · RNZ

Reversals or contradictions

  • National's tax pledge was originally 'no new taxes on working people'. In August 2026 Luxon widened it to 'no new taxes', dropping a bank tax and bed tax that ministers (including Willis) had floated earlier in the year. [1News]
  • In early May 2026 Luxon said National would 'go to the election with another election policy around superannuation and lifting the retirement age for sure'. Its 12 October fiscal plan set no date for raising the age and committed to cross-party talks instead. National says its stance has not changed. [RNZ]

Profile

Role
Prime Minister; Leader of the National Party; Minister for National Security and Intelligence
Electorate / list
MP for Botany (since 2020); contesting Botany
Background
Former chief executive of Air New Zealand and a former Unilever executive. Entered Parliament in 2020, became National leader in November 2021 and Prime Minister in November 2023.

Background source: Wikipedia

Key positions

  • 'No new taxes' (no bank tax, no bed tax); tough welfare obligations, including drug testing for Jobseekers.
  • Has ruled out working with the Opportunity Party because of its land value tax; calls vote-splitting 'insanely stupid'.
  • Campaigns on economic 'turnaround' and 'fixing the basics'.
