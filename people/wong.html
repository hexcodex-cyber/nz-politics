Qiulae Wong

Opportunity: Leader of the Opportunity Party (since November 2025)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We wouldn't want to be part of a government where he is still in that role.”

    24 November 2026 · On Winston Peters remaining foreign minister, which she called "untenable" after his remarks to Green MP Lawrence Xu-Nan; quoted in The Spinoff's 24 November analysis (original remark date not confirmed here). · The Spinoff

  2. “If they are willing to talk and want to talk, then we will have those discussions with them, but if it feels like we're not reaching any agreement or can't move that forward, we will certainly speak to Labour.”

    10 November 2026 · After meeting Luxon on Monday night and Hipkins on Tuesday, saying Opportunity would first honour its commitment to speak with the party that won the most seats. · 1News

  3. “To be honest, it makes my skin crawl, and I don't think someone like that should be standing as a candidate. They don't belong in politics.”

    4 November 2026 · Calling on NZ First candidate Michael Laws to stand down over his remarks about her and Opportunity candidate Kayla Kingdon-Bebb. · RNZ

  4. “It's a win-win for people because regardless of whether you sit on the left or the right, if you care about the things Opportunity advocates for then you're guaranteed to get those things in Parliament regardless of whether it's a Labour or National-led government.”

    27 October 2026 · To supporters in Dunedin's Octagon (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “I have actually felt relatively safe, I've been really supported by the police.”

    22 October 2026 · In Christchurch, asked about candidate safety after the attack on Australian MP Helen Haines (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  6. “I would say I am more optimistic than not that we will be there, but again, we are potentially making history here.”

    13 October 2026 · On Opportunity's rise to 8% in the 1News–Verian poll. · 1News

  7. “We're really encouraged that the general trend is up, and most polls have us well clear of the 5 percent. But obviously, we take nothing for granted and we're out working hard to shore up as many votes as possible”

    9 October 2026 · On polling · RNZ

  8. “We are on track to stand candidates in over thirty electorates this election, because local voices matter.”

    20 March 2026 · Party release announcing first candidates · Opportunity release via Scoop
