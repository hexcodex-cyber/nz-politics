Opportunity: Leader of the Opportunity Party (since November 2025)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We're just going to keep working hard right up until election day. We still need every vote that we can get. We are making history here.”

    29 October 2026 · On Opportunity rising to 7.2% in the RNZ–Reid Research poll. · RNZ

  2. “It's a win-win for people because regardless of whether you sit on the left or the right, if you care about the things Opportunity advocates for then you're guaranteed to get those things in Parliament regardless of whether it's a Labour or National-led government.”

    27 October 2026 · To supporters in Dunedin's Octagon (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  3. “I think where it's gotten to with the whole tit for tat on photos is indicative of the kind of politicking that we're seeing at the moment, rather than focusing on the big issues and the solutions that need to be put forward to solve them.”

    25 October 2026 · Auckland media conference, after saying she had never been photographed with gang members. · 1News

  4. “I think it's completely disrespectful and inappropriate for our political leaders to be talking that way in a country like New Zealand, where we are so proud of our multi culture.”

    21 October 2026 · NZ Herald Powerbrokers' debate, responding to Peters' 'diversity is weakness' remarks (RNZ live blog). · RNZ live blog

  5. “I would say I am more optimistic than not that we will be there, but again, we are potentially making history here.”

    13 October 2026 · On Opportunity's rise to 8% in the 1News–Verian poll. · 1News

  6. “No, I don't believe him. Because he would rather be in government than in opposition. I assume. I can't say that for him.”

    9 October 2026 · Responding to Christopher Luxon ruling out working with Opportunity at TVNZ's leaders' debate · RNZ

  7. “New Zealanders deserve to know who is trying to influence their government, and no one should be able to buy disproportionate political influence.”

    29 September 2026 · Releasing Opportunity's five post-election bottom lines (lobbying regulation and donation caps) · RNZ

    20 March 2026 · Party release announcing first candidates · Opportunity release via Scoop
