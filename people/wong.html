Opportunity: Leader of the Opportunity Party (since November 2025)

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “If they are willing to talk and want to talk, then we will have those discussions with them, but if it feels like we're not reaching any agreement or can't move that forward, we will certainly speak to Labour.”

    10 November 2026 · After meeting Luxon on Monday night and Hipkins on Tuesday, saying Opportunity would first honour its commitment to speak with the party that won the most seats. · 1News

  2. “Ultimately, the decision-making and the thinking behind that, around what gets approved and not, is what's in the best interests of our membership.”

    5 November 2026 · Defending Opportunity's rule letting two board members veto a coalition agreement. · RNZ

  3. “It's becoming a serious consideration for us.”

    28 October 2026 · Asked whether Opportunity would rule out NZ First after Peters defended Ron Mark's 'woke' Manawanui ad. · 1News

    25 October 2026 · Announcing Opportunity's Future Generations Commissioner policy. · RNZ

  5. “We will reach out if he doesn't call us and give him one last chance to have that conversation, and if he doesn't want to, if he really wants to be in opposition, then we will go and speak to Labour next.”

    20 October 2026 · On Luxon ruling Opportunity out, after the 1News–Verian poll. · 1News

  6. “Imagine how many of those most egregious things that have happened in the last three years the Greens could have stopped if they were willing to work with National.”

    13 October 2026 · TVNZ Multi-Party Debate, contrasting Opportunity with the Greens on working across the spectrum. · 1News

  7. “We've said very clearly there are lots of things that we could work with National on... this is just old school politics at play, and come election night, they might change their tune.”

    6 October 2026 · Reacting to Luxon ruling out Opportunity · 1News

    20 March 2026 · Party release announcing first candidates · Opportunity release via Scoop
