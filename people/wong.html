Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “It is always politically difficult, but I think New Zealanders are ready for an honest conversation about superannuation.”

    13 October 2026 · Responding to National's fiscal plan (reported by RNZ) · RNZ
