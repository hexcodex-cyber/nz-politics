Debbie Ngarewa-Packer

Te Pāti Māori: Co-leader of Te Pāti Māori

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “We said this election is bigger than any one party. Our list reflects that commitment.”

    4 October 2026 · On the party's one-person list · RNZ

  2. “There's one plan, two votes; our plan has to be to focus on the candidate votes. We trust you with the party vote we trust and we see your love for our people, for our taiao. We trust that you will choose what is right.”

    16 August 2026 · Speech at Te Pāti Māori campaign launch, Te Poho o Rāwiri Marae, Gisborne · RNZ

  3. “You have the power to bring this government to its knees.”

    16 August 2026 · Campaign launch speech, Gisborne · RNZ

  4. “It's our whānau on the ground. It's not the corporate exploiters, it's not the Federated Farmers, it's not the Fonterras, it's not the BPs, it's not all the mates they're bending to - it's you.”

    16 August 2026 · Campaign launch speech, Gisborne · RNZ

  5. “Even under fast-track legislation designed to push projects through, seabed mining has been found to cause unacceptable harm to the environment, to taonga species, and to tikanga Māori.”

    8 February 2026 · Press release reintroducing her member's bill to ban seabed mining, after the fast-track panel rejected Trans-Tasman Resources' application · Te Pāti Māori release via Scoop

  6. “Protecting our moana and standing with coastal iwi and hapū is why I got into politics in the first place. This Bill is about finishing what we started and making sure these protections are finally locked in for future generations.”

    8 February 2026 · Same seabed-mining release · Te Pāti Māori release via Scoop

None noted in our sources.
