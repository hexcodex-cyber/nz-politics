Julie Anne Genter

Greens: Transport spokesperson; list rank 5

What they've actually said

Direct quotes from 2025–26, newest first. Context lines are ours. Wording is as published in the linked source.

  1. “Years of underinvestment in public transport have left our cities harder to get around without a car.”

    4 October 2026 · Announcing free public transport for children, students and others and a $2 adult fare. · 1News

  2. “Restoring commuter rail will give smaller regions and towns the benefits of more travellers stopping at their town stations, creating more economic opportunities and connecting them to main centres and other towns.”

    3 October 2026 · Announcing the Greens' $4b intercity passenger-rail plan as transport spokesperson. · RNZ

  3. “The train journey from Auckland to Wellington is long, which makes it perfect for an overnight service. All that is needed is new, comfortable sleeper trains and operating funding.”

    3 October 2026 · Same announcement. · RNZ

  4. “Neither Labour nor National is being serious or responsible about how we pay for transport.”

    1 September 2026 · Green release responding to the Government's decision to cancel next year's fuel excise increase. The Greens say the increase should go ahead and be spent on alternatives to driving. · Green Party release

  5. “We do not think a freeze is a good idea, but nor should the extra revenue go into expensive new highways. It should go into frequent bus services, passenger rail, and streets that are safe to walk and cycle on.”

    1 September 2026 · Same release. · Green Party release
